Built on Trust & Transparency

Chicago Diamond Car was founded with a clear vision: to redefine luxury ground travel in Chicago. Over the years, we have grown into a premium transportation company trusted daily by individuals, families, and businesses. We are more than a fleet of vehicles — we are a dedicated team driven by honesty, transparency, and respect.

We provide detailed, upfront quotes with no hidden fees and focus on building long-term relationships. Every trip is unique, which is why we tailor each ride to your exact needs.

Elite Chauffeur Standards

Our chauffeurs are the backbone of Chicago Diamond Car. Each driver undergoes a rigorous background screening process, including fingerprinting, regular drug testing, and a full review of driving history. In addition, our chauffeurs receive advanced training and ongoing evaluations to ensure the highest standards of safety and professionalism.

Whether you're traveling for business, attending an event, or heading to the airport, our chauffeurs provide punctual, discreet, and courteous service every time.
